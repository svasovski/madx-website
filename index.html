MADx

MADx connects Active Directory, Entra ID, Intune, Google Workspace, OneDrive and SharePoint, Mosyle, your HR system, PowerSchool, DNS, DHCP, print servers, file shares and an on-premises AI assistant.

The tools inside MADx

Each tool works on the systems your district already runs. Turn on the ones you use and give each technician only the parts they need.

Find anyone, fix anything

One search covers on-prem AD, Entra ID and Google Workspace. Open a person and everything you can do for them is on one screen.

  • Reset passwords, unlock, enable or disable in a click
  • Create, clone or compare users side by side
  • Move OUs, rename, and manage group memberships
  • Search by department, title or building
  • Force an Entra Connect sync without leaving the page
  • If you choose, let staff outside the tech department reset passwords or look up schedules and attributes, and nothing else

Accounts that build themselves

Point MADx at your HR export and it creates, updates and disables accounts on its own, following rules you set once.

  • Naming and password patterns, department and building codes
  • Groups by location, department, or both together
  • Preview every change before it runs, or do a dry run
  • Handle departures, leaves and returns automatically
  • Emailed results and a temporary-password file for new hires

Every device, whoever manages it

Intune laptops and Mosyle iPads sit side by side, and each person's devices show up on their profile.

  • Lock, restart, locate, rename, retire or wipe
  • Lost mode and play a sound for missing iPads
  • BitLocker recovery keys and LAPS passwords
  • IP addresses filled in from DHCP leases
  • iPad insurance and student fee status at a glance

Entra ID without the portal maze

The Microsoft 365 tasks a district does every week, collected on one board instead of spread across admin centers.

  • Cloud groups, licenses and group-based licensing
  • Conditional Access include and exclude lists
  • Risky users: confirm, dismiss, revoke sessions, reset MFA
  • App registrations, secrets and admin consent requests
  • Google Workspace: suspend, restore, reset, sign out everywhere

When staff leave, their files don't

Search OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams, network shares and Google Shared Drives from one place, and hand a departing person's files to the right person in minutes.

  • Hand over OneDrive and home folders to a manager
  • Choose share in place or copy into their own files
  • Email the new owner links automatically
  • Find anyone-with-the-link and outside sharing, then revoke it

Ask in plain English, on your own AI

MADx connects by API to an AI assistant you host in-house. Your directory data never leaves your network and never trains someone else's model.

  • Look up people, devices, groups and files by asking
  • Run actions in bulk, with a confirmation before anything changes
  • Uses the same permissions as the person asking
  • Works with self-hosted tools like Open WebUI and Ollama

DNS, DHCP and printers, minus the RDP session

See zones, scopes and print queues across your servers, and fix the everyday problems without logging in to each one.

  • DNS zones and records: add, edit and delete, and compare answers across servers
  • Forwarders, aging and scavenging, without opening DNS Manager
  • IPv4 and IPv6 DHCP scopes, reservations, exclusions and leases
  • Search by name, IP or MAC across every server
  • Changes go to the failover primary and replicate
  • Clear a stuck print job or a whole queue
  • A Quick Links board for the vendor portals your team uses

Why I built MADx

Every minute a teacher spends locked out of an account is a minute their students aren't learning. I've spent more than 15 years in K-12 IT trying to give those minutes back.

I started as a part-time building tech, the person who gets pulled into a classroom because the projector won't connect and the sub can't sign in. I worked my way up to technology director, and at every step I saw the same thing: good people stretched across too many buildings, losing hours to jobs that should take seconds. A new hire meant touching half a dozen consoles. A teacher leaving meant a ticket, a script and a scramble to save their files.

None of that time is really ours. It belongs to the classroom. While a tech clicks through five admin portals, a teacher is waiting, and so are their students.

So I built MADx for everyone on the team. The building tech gets a password reset done before the bell rings. The systems admin fixes DNS, DHCP and print queues without logging in to every server. The director sees the whole district in one place and decides exactly who can do what, down to letting the front office reset passwords and nothing else.

And I priced it like a school, not a corporation. MADx isn't priced per seat: one price covers everyone you want to use it, at a fraction of what other companies charge. Every dollar a district doesn't spend on software is a dollar that can reach a classroom.

Our job in IT is to clear the way so teachers can teach and students get their best shot at success. MADx is how I do my part.

Founder of MADx, Miluna LLC

Student data stays in your district

MADx runs on your own Windows Server. There's no MADx cloud and no separate copy of student records.

  • Runs on your servers

    Installed on a domain-joined server in your district and reached over HTTPS only. Connection secrets are encrypted at rest.

  • AI that stays home

    The assistant talks to an AI model you host. Questions and answers never leave your network or train an outside model.

  • Only the access people need

    Nobody gets in by default. Grant pages and actions by AD group, like letting office staff reset passwords for one building and nothing else.

  • Every action logged

    A per-user audit log records who did what and when, kept for as long as you choose.

  • Sign in your way

    Use Microsoft or Google single sign-on with MFA, mapped to the person's AD account.

  • Supports FERPA and COPPA

    Designed to support your district's obligations, with a hardening guide for your IT team.
